01 / SELECTED PROJECT · DOCUMENT AUTOMATION

Document Automation Platform.

Replacing fragmented, manual document workflows with automated services and improved traceability across a complex banking environment.

ROLE · SOFTWARE ENGINEERTECH · JAVA / KAFKA / OPENSHIFTPERIOD · 2024 — PRESENT
THE CONTEXT

A highly manual process.

Mortgage and loan applications relied on manual document handling, legacy systems and external providers. Customers had limited visibility into document status, while teams lacked consistent end-to-end tracking.

The goal was to automate the document lifecycle while integrating new backend services with existing banking systems and external providers.

THE WORK

Engineering across boundaries.

I contributed hands-on Java backend development, Kafka integrations, microservice-based services, technical design, code reviews and infrastructure/deployment work.

With three engineering teams involved, I also took increasing technical ownership: coordinating dependencies and releases, collaborating with Product Owners and architects, and acting as a technical point of contact.

Measurable improvements.

The overall process reached 49.2% automation. Document status became visible within approximately one minute to four hours instead of potentially taking weeks. Classification, extraction and validation of identification documents were automated to take approximately 15–20 seconds.

The platform also improved document traceability, including document status and association with customers and processes.

REFLECTION

Beyond the code.

This work brought together hands-on software development, legacy integration, infrastructure, cross-team delivery and technical leadership. It reinforced my interest in solving complex systems problems while staying close to the code.
